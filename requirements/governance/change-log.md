## 2. Change Request (CR) Protocol
1. **Change Proposal:** Submit formal CR with business rationale and target scope.
2. **Impact Analysis:** Cross-reference RTM to identify all affected specifications, contracts, and test suites.
3. **Review & Approval:** Stakeholder sign-off prior to codebase modification.
4. **Specification Update:** Update `spec.md`, `api-contract.md`, and `acceptance-criteria.md`.
5. **Governance Sign-off:** Update RTM and log entry in `change-log.md`.
